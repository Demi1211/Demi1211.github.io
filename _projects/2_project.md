This project examines how readers process moral and semantic violations, with a focus on N400 and P600 event-related potential responses. The work was published in *Brain Sciences* in 2026 with Jian Meng, Yuling Zhong, Xiaodong Xu, and Edith Kaan.

[Read the paper](https://doi.org/10.3390/brainsci16040375).
