At UF, I taught ENC 1101 Expository and Argumentative Writing across four semesters, plus ENC 3246 Professional Communication for Engineers and ENC 1102 Argument and Persuasion in Summer 2026. My courses emphasize argument, research, audience awareness, multimodal communication, and generous but concrete revision feedback.

I also taught ENC 2256 Writing in the Disciplines, served as a writing coach for ENC 3254, and worked as a teaching assistant for LIN 1140 Language and Emotion and LIN 2008 Language and Computers.
