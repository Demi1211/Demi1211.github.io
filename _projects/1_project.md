This project asks how producing code-switches shapes cognitive control. I designed the main E-Prime production task, conflict adaptation scheme, bilingual language-profile materials, verbal fluency task, coding workflow, and analysis plan.

The study tracks production behaviors such as incorrect switching, self-corrections, and hesitations, and connects those behaviors to individual differences in bilingual experience. I also manage IRB materials and mentor two undergraduate research assistants on weekly coding and project workflows.
