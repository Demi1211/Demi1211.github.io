This project asks how producing code-switches shapes cognitive control. I led this project and designed the main E-Prime production task, conflict adaptation scheme, bilingual language-profile materials, verbal fluency task, coding workflow, and analysis plan.

The study tracks production behaviors such as incorrect switching, self-corrections, and hesitations, and connects those behaviors to individual differences in bilingual experience. I managed the IRB materials and mentored two undergraduate research assistants, Emiliana Pulido and María Josefina Estrada, who are co-authors on the resulting paper, now in press in *Bilingualism: Language and Cognition*.
