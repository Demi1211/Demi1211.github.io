My dissertation work is connected to the NSF-funded project [Predictive processing in naturalistic language comprehension through EEG and computational modeling](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2518249), a collaboration between Jonathan Brennan at the University of Michigan and Edith Kaan at the University of Florida.

The project studies EEG data from bilingual speakers of English, Mandarin, and Spanish and develops computational models of second-language processing in naturalistic comprehension. My dissertation builds from this space: how bilingual minds make predictions while listening and reading in a second language, and how brain responses can be linked to model-based measures of language expectation.
