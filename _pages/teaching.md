### ENC 1102: Argument and Persuasion
_Instructor of Record, Summer B 2026_  
A first-year writing course focused on argument, research, evidence, and revision. Students practice building persuasive claims for public and academic audiences.

### ENC 3246: Professional Communication for Engineers
_Instructor of Record, Summer A 2026_  
A professional communication course for engineering students, emphasizing audience-centered technical writing, document design, workplace genres, and clear communication for collaborative engineering contexts.

### LIN 2008: Language and Computers
_Teaching Assistant (Instructor: Dr. Sarah Moeller), Spring 2026_  
An introductory course on how computers represent and process human language, from writing systems and speech to spell-checkers and other language technologies. I led discussion sections and graded assignments.

### LIN 1140: Language and Emotion
_Teaching Assistant / Grader (Instructor: Dr. Eleonora Rossi), Fall 2025_  
An undergraduate linguistics course on how language expresses, shapes, and responds to emotion. I graded assignments, supported course logistics, and helped students connect linguistic concepts to everyday communication.

### ENC 2256: Writing in the Disciplines
_Instructor of Record, Summer B 2025_  
A discipline-based writing course that helps students analyze genre conventions, write for specific audiences, and revise professional documents with attention to clarity and purpose.

### ENC 3254: Professional Writing in the Discipline
_Writing Coach, Summer A 2024_  
A professional writing course centered on clarity, organization, genre awareness, and disciplinary communication. I provided individualized feedback, led workshops and office hours, and created supplementary writing resources.

### ENC 1101: Expository and Argumentative Writing
_Instructor of Record, August 2023 - May 2025_  
A first-year writing course on critical thinking, research, persuasive writing, and multimodal communication. I taught four semesters of face-to-face and online sections, reaching more than 200 students.
