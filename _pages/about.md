Hello world! This is Demi from the University of Florida, born and raised in [Anyang, China](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w83Op5ddaiw). I am a Linguistics Ph.D. student working in psycholinguistics, bilingualism, and computational approaches to language. Under the guidance of [Dr. Edith Kaan](https://blab.lin.ufl.edu/directory/dr-edith-kaan/), I study how code-switching production relates to cognitive control, and how the brain responds to moral and semantic violations. I am also pursuing a Computer Science Ph.D. minor with [Dr. Bonnie Dorr](https://nlp.cise.ufl.edu/~bonniejdorr) and am a member of the [NLP & Culture Lab](https://nlp.cise.ufl.edu/), where my work connects cognitive modeling, topic modeling, and multilingual NLP.

Adventure is in my soul. I travel on my black Cannondale gravel bike, and recent solo travels have taken me to Mexico, Peru, Egypt, the UAE, and the Orinoco River region in Colombia. As an active member of the [Warmshowers](https://www.warmshowers.org/user/login?destination=/user/279774/member-reviews) community, I host fellow cyclists and enjoy meeting generous people who host me in return. See the [blog](/blog/) for my River Orinoco journal.
